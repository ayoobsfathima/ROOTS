Trajectory Explorer

Enter a child's visit history — age plus CARS2-ST item scores (1–4, in 0.5 steps), grouped by the three published CARS2-ST factors (Campbell et al., 2026). The tool personalizes that child's developmental pace and timing on a shared disease-course timeline, then plots their predicted trajectory for any item against the population average.

Prototype built on synthetic data (n=300), calibrated to match a published CARS2-ST factor structure but not fit to real patients. Not for clinical decision-making. CARS2-ST item content is copyrighted (Schopler et al., 2010, WPS) and is not reproduced here — items are shown only by number and their published short domain label.
